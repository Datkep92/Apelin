# Apelin 0.4.11

This release gives the conversation plan bar a live progress track, showing plan completion at a glance and animating as steps finish.

- The plan bar now draws a progress track and coloured fill whose width follows how many steps are done.
- The fill changes colour with the plan state: blue while active, orange when paused, red when cancelled, animating smoothly as steps complete.
- The progress track sits inside the plan bar and stays out of VoiceOver's path over the steps.

Install the matching rootless package from `SHA256SUMS`.
