# Release 0.4.24

Adds a compact mode to the plan progress bar: the whole bar can be hidden into a small circular progress badge showing the percentage, toggled from the header or by tapping the badge.

- Plan progress bar can now be collapsed into a small circular badge with a percentage.
- Tap the eye button in the header to hide the bar, or tap the badge to bring it back.
- Hidden state is not persisted and resets when the plan clears.
- No change to plan logic, step ordering, or public behavior.

Use the rootless arm64 build on iOS 16+, or the roothide build where applicable. Verify package SHA256 from SHA256SUMS before installing.
