# Release 0.4.17

Reverts the expanded process board ordering so steps follow the actual process chronology: completed/older steps stay at the top area and the newest step appears at the bottom, as a natural process feed.

- Show expanded process steps in plan order (older first, newest last).
- Keep step-number mapping and step-detail callback unchanged.
- Keep compact plan bar styling and action buttons from 0.4.16.

Use the rootless arm64 build on iOS 16+, or the roothide build where applicable. Verify package SHA256 from SHA256SUMS before installing.
