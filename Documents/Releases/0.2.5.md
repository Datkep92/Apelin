Apelin 0.2.5 lists a reply's thoughts and tool calls step by step, marks where a chat was compacted, and says a lost connection in one plain sentence.

- A chat that lost its connection showed a long technical error, starting with `Error Domain=NSURLErrorDomain Code=-1009`. It now says only "The Internet connection appears to be offline.", in earlier chats too.
- Tapping a reply's summary opened one long page of every thought and tool call. It now lists the steps, each with what it was and one line of detail; tap a step to read it whole.
- After Compact…, nothing in the chat showed that it had been compacted. A Compacted row now marks the place; tap it to read the summary the chat goes on from.
- A Claude chat whose context had filled could stop with "context is too large and compaction failed: Anthropic compaction returned no summary". When Anthropic sends back no summary, Apelin now writes one itself and the chat goes on.
- Before iOS 26, the button that scrolls to the latest message was a flat grey circle. It is now blurred like the bars around it.
- On an iPad, Open in Irisin… under the Repository Card was squeezed against the bottom of the sheet. It is in the card's `…` menu now, and the card has the whole sheet.
- Apelin's page in your package manager showed only its icon at the top. It now shows the app at work on an iPhone.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
