Apelin 0.1.8 lets you compact a chat yourself, between replies.

- A long chat could only be compacted when the agent ran out of room on its own, and when that failed there was no way to try again or see why. A chat's `…` menu now has Compact…, available once the reply has finished: your agent continues from a summary of the earlier messages, the messages stay in the chat, you can cancel while it runs, and a failure shows the model provider's own reason.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, and Irisin 4.7.3 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
