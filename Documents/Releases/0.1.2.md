Apelin 0.1.2 speaks eleven more languages.

- Use Apelin in Arabic, Chinese (Traditional), French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Spanish or Vietnamese, as well as English and Chinese (Simplified). It follows your device's language.
- In Arabic, the sidebar, the message field and the conversation read right to left.
- A new chat's name now follows the language you use, and a chat started before you changed language still takes its name from your first message.
- Day headings in the sidebar start with a capital letter in French, Italian and Spanish.
- Deleting a chat or a project now also removes its notifications.
- When a widget has no chat to show, it now says there are no messages yet, rather than no chats.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, and Irisin 4.7.3 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
