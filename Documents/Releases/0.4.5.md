Apelin 0.4.5 stops a paused plan and keeps the plan bar steady while a turn runs.

- Pause and End in the plan bar now halt the work currently in progress instead of only marking the plan paused, so the model stops acting away from a plan you asked to stand still; type "continue" to resume from there.
- Stepping through a plan no longer rebuilds its bar on every refresh, so expanded steps, scroll position and pending input stay put.
- Changing the plan bar's height now animates, instead of jumping the conversation list.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
