Apelin 0.3.4 lets a model provider be deleted from Settings instead of only signed out of.

- In Settings ▸ Model Providers, a provider configured with its own key or endpoint had no way to be removed: signing out brought it back as a row waiting for a key, and the only swipe was on a configuration that had never been saved. Each saved configuration now offers **Delete**, in its `...` menu beside Sign Out and by swiping its row, and asks once before it goes.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
