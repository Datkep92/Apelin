# Release 0.4.21

The plan bar above the composer gets a more modern look and smoother motion: finished steps are tinted, the expand chevron rotates, and step rows fade in when the bar rebuilds.

- Completed steps now show a subtle accent background so finished work is easy to spot at a glance.
- The expand/collapse chevron animates its rotation instead of snapping.
- Step rows fade in when the plan bar rebuilds, instead of appearing all at once.
- All of these animations turn off when Reduce Motion is on.

Use the rootless arm64 build on iOS 16+, or the roothide build where applicable. Verify package SHA256 from SHA256SUMS before installing.
