Apelin 0.3.3 adds long-press *Copy + Delete* to user message bubbles, fixes the confirmation for *Copy + Delete* to use localized literals from `Localizable.xcstrings`, and `ci.yml` now caches Xcode build products (intermediates and packages) by source hash.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
