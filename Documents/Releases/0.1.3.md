Apelin 0.1.3 lets ChatGPT Codex answer faster and gives the agent a firmer grasp of your project and device.

- With a ChatGPT Codex model, a new Speed control beside the reasoning effort chooses Standard, Fast or, on the models that offer it, Ultrafast. It appears only for models that offer a faster speed, and Apelin remembers your choice with the model.
- A reply that broke off partway, when the connection to the model dropped, ended the turn with an error; Apelin now asks again, as long as nothing had happened yet because of it.
- The agent sometimes could not read Apelin's built-in guides, refused a command it had been given, or said memory had changed when it was saving a new memory; it now treats a field it left empty as left out.
- Asked about a project's build settings or a Theos tweak, the agent guessed field names or reached for Theos; it now knows every field of a project's build description, rewrites a Theos tweak's hooks as plain Objective-C, and keeps its hooks from clashing with other tweaks.
- The agent no longer goes looking for build tools the device does not have, and on RootHide no longer hands the compilers paths they cannot open.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, and Irisin 4.7.3 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
