Apelin 0.2.3 lets the agent look into what your device has with icli's new probe, and points your package repository to Irisin alone.

- When no command answered a question about the device — whether a class or a private method exists, what a framework reports — the agent could only guess. It now runs a short script through `icli probe` to find out, reading only; changes still go through its tools and your approvals.
- The Repository Card had no way to add the source on the device you were holding. Open in Irisin… under the card now adds it there.
- Your package repository's page was a plain list with buttons for Sileo and Zebra. It is now the repository's card, the same as in Apelin, with its QR code and Open in Irisin… under it, and a new repository uses Apelin's icon. Settings points to Irisin alone too.
- A repository made with an earlier Apelin kept its old page. Settings ▸ Git ▸ Update Repository… brings its page, README, icon and workflow up to date, after warning that it overwrites them; your packages and depictions stay.
- A saved Repository Card lost its background and no longer looked like a card. It is saved on a grey page now, and a chosen icon can be removed again from the `…` menu.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
