# Release 0.4.20

The plan bar above the composer now keeps finished steps at the top, so a completed step moves up instead of staying where it started.

- The plan bar listed steps in their original order, so finished steps and the one running were mixed together. Completed steps now group at the top, with the remaining steps below in their original order; each step keeps its original number for updates and the detail sheet.
- The progress fill could stay the wrong width when the bar appeared before its track was laid out, or after the track changed size. It now remembers its ratio and recomputes the fill whenever the track is measured again.
- The in-place refresh that relabels steps without rebuilding now only happens when no step changed status, so a step that just completed rebuilds and moves up instead of being relabelled where it stood.

Use the rootless arm64 build on iOS 16+, or the roothide build where applicable. Verify package SHA256 from SHA256SUMS before installing.
