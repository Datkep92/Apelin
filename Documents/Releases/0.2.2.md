Apelin 0.2.2 makes a card anyone can scan to add your package repository, and lets you publish from a GitHub organization.

- Sharing your package repository meant sending its address for someone to type. Settings ▸ Git ▸ Repository Card… now shows a card with a QR code: pointing the Camera at it opens Irisin and adds the source. Choose its icon and title from the `…` menu, and Save puts it in Photos.
- Create Distribution Repository could only make a repository on your own account, and Link listed only your own repositories. You can now create one in an organization you belong to, and link any repository you can push to, yours or an organization's.
- In a confirmation that removes something, both buttons had a blue outline around the red. They are red throughout now.
- Cancel on a progress card was taller than the buttons of every other alert. It is the same height now.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.9.2 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
