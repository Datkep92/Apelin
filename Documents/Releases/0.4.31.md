# 0.4.31

Adds quick-reply buttons under the composer when the assistant ends a message with a question, so common answers (Có, Không, Tiếp tục, Giải thích) can be sent with one tap instead of typing.

- New composer section `ComposerQuickReplySection` with a row of reply buttons.
- Quick replies appear only when no approval is pending and the conversation is idle.
- Tapping a reply sends its text as the user's next message.
