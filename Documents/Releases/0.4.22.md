# Release 0.4.22

Cold app and service launches are more robust: Apelin waits long enough for its background service to come up and no longer gives up and asks you to retry messages.

- The app now allows up to 60 seconds for the background service's cold start, instead of falling back after 10 seconds.
- The background service no longer reads every superseded conversation task log at each startup, removing a common cause of very slow launches.
- Startup now tolerates a longer first hello reply (10 seconds per attempt) before retrying.

Use the rootless arm64 build on iOS 16+, or the roothide build where applicable. Verify package SHA256 from SHA256SUMS before installing.
