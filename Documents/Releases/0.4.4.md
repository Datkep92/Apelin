Apelin 0.4.4 shows conversations and the detail pane sooner at launch.

- Conversation and recent chat records are read from disk first, shown immediately, and host synchronization continues in the background.
- Service unavailability during that window no longer delays the conversation list.
- Internal reconciliation keeps existing behavior once the host answers.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
