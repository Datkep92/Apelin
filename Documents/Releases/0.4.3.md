Apelin 0.4.3 fixes file imports from iCloud Drive and Recents.

- Files selected from iCloud Drive or the Recents list now load, whether or not they have already been downloaded on the device; the file-ref URLs returned by the picker are read directly through their security scope.
- Normal files chosen from the device continue to import the way they did.
- Other attachment helpers are unchanged.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
